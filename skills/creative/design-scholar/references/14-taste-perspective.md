## Taste Is Not Innate — It Is Built

- Beginners already HAVE killer taste — that's why they entered creative work. What they lack is ability. Gap normal (2 years behind). Most quit in gap (Ira Glass). Only closed by **shipped volume on deadline** (every week finish one story), for years.

## Glass Prescription (Verbatim Core)

"Nobody tells this to people who are beginners... All of us who do creative work, we get into it because we have good taste. But there is this gap. For the first couple years you make stuff, it's just not that good... But your taste... is still killer. And your taste is why your work disappoints you. A lot of people never get past this phase, they quit. The most important thing you can do is do a lot of work. Put yourself on a deadline so that every week you will finish one story. It is ONLY by going through a volume of work that you will close that gap..." Ira Glass. Took longer than anyone he knew. Fight through.

## How Taste Is Built: Literacy → Taste → Style

- Chain: Literacy (grammar: composition/hierarchy/contrast/pacing, lets you UNDERSTAND) → Taste (inner filter, quiet yes/no, helps you DECIDE) → Style (trace you leave, pattern of choices). Never reverse.
- Two things build taste: TO LOOK + TO REFLECT. Notice the "wow" and ask WHY. Collect→curate→edit→prune for YEARS; saved images become library-compass. Moodboards are thinking tools (if it moves idea forward, keep; if just loud, cut). Durability test: still yes in 2 years? Tasteful = specific, calm, inevitable. Tasteless = decoration without context/why. With AI, taste is scarcest skill (Savee, 1.2M+ curators) — visual library built by collecting.

## Maeda — Laws of Simplicity (iPod era, 100pp)

- 10 Laws; taste operation is Law 10 "Subtract the obvious, add the meaningful" + Law 4 "Knowledge makes everything simpler" = volume thesis. Keys: AWAY/OPEN/POWER. Reduction is meaningful choice, not removal.

## Pixel Discipline as Taste Gym

- Lospec 586 tutorials: Pixel Logic (Azzi), Creating Pixel Art (cure), Palettes (Sadface), Shading (Medeiros); constraints (32×32, 4–16 colors) teach clusters, selective outline, palette control, dithering — "fewer colors = better." Art Foundation (PixelSchool.org) via strict constraint. Every pixel is a decision — 16×16 weapon proves it (see 12-builder-culture).

## Builder vs Craftsman

- Builder delivers style without literacy/taste — technically correct but lifeless, skips volume, ages overnight. Craftsman closes gap via weekly shipping, deliberate curation, pixel-level judgment, rationalizing every decision then trusting instinct. This is perspective without sentience — learned, not gifted.

## Protocol (Adopt Now)

- Weekly shipped volume on deadline (Glass). Collect→prune visual library (Savee/Readymag). Test every decision at 400% + 16px before shipping. Ask "why does this wow/disappoint?" then fix one pixel.
