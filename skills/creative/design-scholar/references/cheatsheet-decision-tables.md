# Cheatsheet — Decision Tables

Load this when you need a quick token/type/motion choice without re-reading chapters.

## Canvas Choice

- SaaS/product (Linear): `#08090a` / `#0f1011` / `rgba(255,255,255,.02)` cards — dark is the whitespace.
- Service/local (barber): `#FFFBF7` warm paper — feels like a shop, not an app.
- Editorial/salon: `#f5f4ed` parchment — human companion, not powerful tool.

## Accent Choice

- One accent only. Barber warm = `#EA580C`. SaaS indigo = `#5e6ad2`. Never both.

## Radius Choice

- Buttons 6 / cards 8 / panels 12 / pills 9999 (chips only).

## Motion Choice

- Burst `.016`, reveal `inView -40px .45`, hover `420/18`, logo `480/16 1.08`.

## Logo Choice

- Pole: `10×24 rx5 white stroke .6` in `22×34 transparent`, stripes `2px rotate -14` clipped exactly. Scissors alt: `20px stroke 1.7` in `36px black tile`.

## Anti-Slop Ship Checklist (5s)

`tel 0 masked` / `s3-media 0` / `gap double 0` / `header one topin` / `nav one` / `JSON-LD schema.org` / `clipPath match` / `no fake booking` / `no dot graph`.
