## Dieter Rams — 10 Principles for Good Design (late 1970s, Vitsœ)

Gate every feature through 3 filters: **useful + understandable + honest** — else cut. Full 10: Innovative (tech enables, not end), Useful (functional+psych+aesthetic), Aesthetic (beauty = usefulness), Understandable (self-explanatory, T 1000), Unobtrusive (tool not sculpture), Honest (no inflated promise), Long-lasting (anti-fashion, 606 1959→ still sold), Thorough to last detail (precision = respect, ET 66), Environmentally friendly (conserve + visual pollution), As little design as possible (Less but better, Weniger aber besser). Lineage: Ive/Apple is heir (iPod→T3, iOS clock→Braun). Credo: indifference to people = cardinal sin.

## Don Norman — DOET (1988/2013)

Thesis: failures are missing intuitive guidance, not user blame. Key fix (2013 p.9): **affordance** (what's possible) ≠ **signifier** (what signals it: flat plate = push). 4 principles: Visibility, Conceptual model, Good mapping (knobs→burners), Feedback (immediate, informative). 7 stages: Execution (goal→intention→specify→execute) / Evaluation (perceive→interpret→evaluate). Gulfs: Execution (intent→action) bridged by signifiers/constraints/mappings; Evaluation (perception→understanding) bridged by feedback/visibility. Constraints taxonomy: physical/semantic/cultural/logical. Anecdote: Italy projector 14 min + 5 helpers proves undiscoverable design punishes everyone.

## Refactoring UI (Wathan/Schoger, 2018, 50 ch)

Mantra: **tactics not talent** — naming the move makes it visible. Hierarchy: size isn't everything — weight/contrast/color (de-emphasize to emphasize, muted grey-500 secondary vs saturated primary-600). Never grey text on colored bg. Layout: start with too much whitespace (double outer gutters), establish 4/8px scale, don't fill full screen, grids overrated, avoid ambiguous spacing (identical or clearly different). Color: ditch hex for HSL, greys 8-10 + primaries 5-10 shades 100–900, greys needn't be grey (tinted). Palette recipe: greys 8-10 + primary 1-2 hues 5-10 shades + accents via alert-component test. Depth: single light source, umbra+penumbra shadows. Finishing: supercharge defaults, accent borders (left-border callouts), fewer borders (replace with shadow/bg/whitespace).

## Weinschenk — 100 Things (2011/2020, 10×10)

SEE: vision reconstructs, peripheral catches motion/contrast — animate only what matters. READ: saccades + 200-250ms fixations, 45-72 char line, scan not read, front-load keywords. REMEMBER: STM ~30s, 4±1 chunks, recognition > recall. THINK: mental models drive expectations, satisfice (first good enough). FOCUS: one conscious stream, attention selective + scarce, novelty via motion/size/contrast. MOTIVATE: intrinsic (autonomy/mastery/purpose/curiosity/belonging) > extrinsic; flow = goal+challenge≈skill+feedback. SOCIAL: mimicry/conformity/authority/social proof + faces/gaze pull. FEEL: positive broadens, negative narrows, anxiety degrades — reduce uncertainty + give control. MISTAKES: slip vs mistake, confirmation bias, easy undo + safe defaults. DECIDE: Hick (time ~ log2 choices, fewer choices faster) + Fitts (time ~ distance/size, corners = infinite targets) + information scent + progressive disclosure.

## Airey + Paul Rand lineage

Airey pipeline: Research → Exploration (30+ pen sketches, first 10 clichés) → Refinement (B&W first) → Presentation (2-3 with rationale) → Delivery (guidelines). 5 tests: Distinctive, Visible (1" / 16px), Usable (1-color/reversed), Memorable (redraw), Universal. Rand: logo identifies, not sells; Simple via repeated reduction; timeless > trend; NeXT 28° 100pp spec = craft signal. Through-line (all 5): **reduce to essentials, make remainder unmistakable** — Rams × Rand × Refactoring × Weinschenk × Norman converge.
